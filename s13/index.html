The highest priority is preserving their exact identity.
Keep the face, facial structure, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone, body type, and overall likeness exactly as in the reference photo.
Keep the hairstyle exactly as in the reference photo — same hair length, same hair color, same texture, same bangs or parting.
Do not beautify, age, or alter the face in any way.

Clothing may be changed, but the person must remain clearly recognizable as the same individual.
Dress them in a stylish, modern outfit suitable for a cool dance-performance lead character.
The outfit should look natural, fashionable, and easy to move in.

Show the same person consistently across all panels.
Use a clean light gray or off-white studio background.
Use neutral, even studio lighting.
Do not use dramatic colored lighting, nightclub lighting, crowds, or outdoor backgrounds.

Include these panels:
1. front full-body view
2. 3/4 full-body view
3. side full-body view
4. back full-body view
5. front close-up portrait
6. 3/4 close-up portrait
7. relaxed standing pose
8. simple dance-ready pose

Important:
- preserve the exact same face in every panel
- preserve the exact same hairstyle and hair color in every panel
- keep anatomy realistic and proportions consistent

- no text except small simple view labels if needed

Style: realistic photo reference sheet, high detail, identity-consistent character turnaround, suitable for AI video generation.
